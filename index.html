Our Story

Every piece has a soul. Yours is worth crafting.

New A.K Furniture began in a small workshop in Jaipur with one belief — that beautiful things should also be honest things. No hidden chemicals, no exploited labour, no short-lived trends.

Today, we work with 180+ artisans across India, transforming reclaimed teak, salvaged mango wood, and responsibly harvested sheesham into furniture you'll want to pass down.

Responsibly Sourced Wood

FSC-certified timber, reclaimed ship wood, and salvaged heritage teak.

Fair Wages, Always

Our craftsmen earn 40% above industry average, with health cover and year-round work.

Built to Last Generations

Traditional joinery, no cheap screws. Our pieces are designed to outlive us all.

Our Craft

From forest to your living room

Four careful steps. Hundreds of hours. One piece that will outlive trends, seasons, and maybe even you.

01

Sourcing

We salvage teak from old havelis, reclaimed ship wood from Gujarat ports, and FSC mango wood from certified farms.

02

Seasoning

Every plank air-dries for 90 days, then is kiln-cured to precise moisture levels. This is why our furniture never warps.

03

Handcrafting

Joint by joint, our artisans shape each piece using traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery. No shortcuts.

04

Finishing

Natural oils, beeswax, and plant-based stains only. No polyurethane, no formaldehyde.

What We Stand For

Four promises we never break

100% Natural

No plastic, no MDF, no harmful varnishes. Only solid wood and honest materials.

Fair Made

Our artisans are salaried, insured, and respected. Every piece is signed with their name.

Zero Waste

Offcuts become coasters, sawdust fuels our kilns, leftover stains are composted.

Built Forever

10-year structural warranty and free lifetime repairs. If it breaks, we fix it.

Questions

Everything you want to know

How long does a custom piece take to make?

Custom orders typically take 3–6 weeks depending on complexity and finish.

Is the wood really sustainable?

Yes. 94% of our wood is either reclaimed or FSC-certified. For every tree we use, we plant 5 more.

Do you deliver across India?

We deliver to 45+ cities with our own logistics team — never third-party couriers.

What is your warranty policy?

Every piece comes with a 10-year structural warranty plus free lifetime repairs.

Can I visit your workshop?

Absolutely. Our Jaipur workshop welcomes visitors by appointment.
